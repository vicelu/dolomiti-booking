The cottage

Ciasa Irma

Ciasa means “house” in Ladin, the old language of the Fassa valley. Ciasa Irma sits on Strada de Gardecia in Pera di Fassa — the road that climbs toward the Catinaccio (Rosengarten) massif, which glows pink at sunset. Just a 10min ride to Sella ronda and 3min to the nearest lifts :) Inside there's a wood-panelled stube with a bunk bed, a double bedroom, a kitchen with an old wood-burning stove for shared dinners, and a balcony that looks straight across the valley to the slopes.
